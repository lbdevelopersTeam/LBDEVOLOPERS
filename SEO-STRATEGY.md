# LB CodeBase SEO Strategy

## Strategic objective

Generate qualified enquiries for web development, ecommerce engineering, website redesign, and practical AI automation from Pakistan and international markets without diluting the brand into a generic “full-service digital agency.”

## Recommended information architecture

```text
/
├── /services
│   ├── /services/web-development
│   ├── /services/shopify-development
│   ├── /services/wordpress-development
│   ├── /services/ai-automation
│   ├── /services/portals-dashboards
│   └── /services/website-redesign
├── /portfolio
│   └── /portfolio/:case-study
├── /about
│   └── /team/:member
├── /blog
│   └── /blog/:article
└── /contact
```

The existing dynamic service, portfolio, blog, and team routes already provide a useful base. The next SEO expansion should give the most commercially important service families stable, descriptive URLs instead of placing every offer on one long page.

## On-page rules

- One clear H1 per indexable page.
- Put the page’s primary service and market in the title, H1, first paragraph, and relevant internal link text.
- Write useful descriptions for buyers, not keyword lists.
- Add a specific CTA to every service and case-study page.
- Link service pages to relevant case studies and link case studies back to the service that delivered them.
- Use real screenshots, team expertise, implementation details, and measurable outcomes.

## Structured-data plan

- Homepage: `ProfessionalService`/`Organization`.
- Service pages: `Service` with provider and service type.
- Case studies: `Article` or `WebPage` with a clear author/provider relationship.
- Team pages: `Person` with `worksFor` and verified social profiles.
- Blog posts: `BlogPosting` with author, date, headline, image, and publisher.
- Do not add commercial `FAQPage` markup merely for visibility; Google restricts FAQ rich results largely to authoritative government and health sites.

## 90-day content roadmap

### Days 1–30: foundation

1. Publish stable service landing pages for web development, Shopify, WordPress, and AI automation.
2. Add the homepage Organization/ProfessionalService markup and consistent social metadata.
3. Connect Search Console, submit `/sitemap.xml`, and verify `/robots.txt`.
4. Improve every portfolio item with a real outcome, technology, and delivery context.

### Days 31–60: commercial coverage

1. Publish “Website Development Company in Pakistan” as a focused service page.
2. Publish “Shopify Development Agency in Pakistan.”
3. Publish “AI Automation and n8n Workflows for Businesses.”
4. Add internal links from the homepage, services hub, portfolio, and blog.

### Days 61–90: authority

1. Publish a website-cost guide grounded in LB CodeBase’s actual scope and process.
2. Publish a Shopify vs WooCommerce decision guide for Pakistani businesses.
3. Publish an n8n lead-routing implementation guide with a real architecture diagram.
4. Earn independent mentions through client launches, founder profiles, technical communities, and relevant directories.

## KPIs

- Qualified organic enquiries, not traffic alone.
- Search Console impressions and CTR for each keyword cluster.
- Indexed valid URLs and sitemap discovery.
- Organic entrances to service and case-study pages.
- Conversion rate from organic service-page sessions.
- Mobile LCP, INP, and CLS after the video-heavy hero is optimized.

## Important limitation

No public tool can provide your real Google keyword volume, ranking, or conversion data without Search Console/Keyword Planner access. The keyword priorities above are evidence-based commercial hypotheses and should be refined using your own query and enquiry data.
